# Enrollment-first invoice workflow

## Recommended operational flow

1. Open **Admin → Students → Student**.
2. Open the active enrollment or click **Billing / Invoice**.
3. In **Billing & Invoice**:
   - if no package exists, click **Add fee package**; Finance opens with this enrollment preselected;
   - INR packages are shown as Indian collections and do not offer Razorpay;
   - foreign-currency packages show **Invoice required**.
4. Review customer billing address, FX rate, INR equivalent and export/LUT details.
5. Confirm the export/LUT review checkbox.
6. Click **Create Razorpay draft**.
7. From the same enrollment, issue, send email, open or cancel the invoice.
8. Use **Admin → Razorpay Invoices** as the central ledger/receivables dashboard rather than the primary creation workflow.
